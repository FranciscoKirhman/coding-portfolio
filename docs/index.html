About

Welcome. I’m an Engineer in Molecular Biotechnology from Universidad de Chile, working where research evidence, clinical research and medical education meet.

My research spans genomic bioinformatics, biomaterials and microbiology, with three peer-reviewed articles. Each one has a plain-language summary below.

At Johnson & Johnson MedTech I coordinated ten national medical education programs for approximately 260 healthcare professionals under HCC and HCBI compliance frameworks, and developed the Future Leaders curriculum with academic leaders.

My clinical research training includes dual GCP certification, a PUC Chile Diploma in Clinical Study Management, and reviewing research proposals before ethics committee submission at Clínica Indisa.
